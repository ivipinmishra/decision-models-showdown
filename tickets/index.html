Support Ticket Router

Can a decision model route a messy, free-text support ticket? Each provider gets the ticket and three questions in one batched request. The models only answer. Your code decides where the ticket goes.

InputA customer's message for TaskHarbor

Choose a ticket

Pick one of 20 labeled samples or write your own. The label is what a human expects.
